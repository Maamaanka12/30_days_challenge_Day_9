01 / About the house

An atelier,
not a store.

VESTIRE started with one cutting table and a simple complaint: menswear either costs a fortune or falls apart. We draft our own blocks, buy cloth from two family mills, and fit every silhouette on real bodies — sizes 34 to 52.

  • Drafted, not copiedOriginal blocks graded by hand for shoulders that sit right.
  • Cloth with papers120s wool, 14oz selvedge, brushed cotton — every roll traceable.
  • Worn 5 years, not 5 washesReinforced seams, horn buttons, free repairs for life.

03 / How it’s made — a real sequence

Measure. Cut. Sew.

  1. Step 1

    Measure twice

    Chest, shoulder, sleeve and stance — 14 points taken in-store or by video call.

  2. Step 2

    Cut once

    Your block is drafted in chalk on the cloth. Canvassed chest, never fused.

  3. Step 3

    Sewn to stay

    Reinforced seams, horn buttons, hand-set collar. Pressed, fitted, finished in 48h.
